1. Income Tax & ITR

  • Income heads: Salary, House Property, Business/Profession, Capital Gains and Other Sources.
  • Understand residential status, gross total income, deductions and total income.
  • Choose the applicable ITR form based on taxpayer type and income sources.
  • Keep AIS, TIS, Form 26AS, bank statements and supporting documents ready.
Tip: Tax rates, deductions, due dates and ITR forms can change. Verify the current year on the official Income Tax portal.
Official Income Tax Portal ↗

2. GST

  • GST registration, taxable supply, place of supply and tax invoice basics.
  • GSTR-1 is used for reporting outward supplies; GSTR-3B is a summary return with tax payment.
  • Input Tax Credit is subject to eligibility, documentation, receipt of supply and other statutory conditions.
  • Learn e-Invoice, e-Way Bill, credit/debit notes and reconciliation.
Working practice: Reconcile purchase registers with GSTR-2B before claiming eligible ITC.
Official GST Portal ↗

3. TDS & TCS

  • TDS means Tax Deducted at Source and applies to specified payments subject to the Income-tax Act.
  • Identify the correct section, rate, threshold, timing and PAN requirement before deduction.
  • Deposit deducted tax within the applicable statutory timeline and file the relevant quarterly statement.
  • Issue applicable certificates such as Form 16/16A and reconcile with Form 26AS/AIS.
Income Tax Official Portal ↗

4. Accounting Fundamentals

  • Accounting equation: Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
  • Journal entries record transactions; ledger accounts classify them account-wise.
  • Trial balance checks arithmetical agreement of debit and credit balances.
  • Profit & Loss reports performance for a period; Balance Sheet presents financial position at a point in time.
  • Understand depreciation, accruals, prepayments, provisions and closing stock.
Course: Use the Accountant Academy section for structured learning and the online exam.

5. MSME / Udyam

  • Udyam Registration is the government registration framework for eligible MSMEs.
  • Keep PAN, Aadhaar and other required business details ready as applicable.
  • Use only the official government website for registration and updates.
Official Udyam Portal ↗

6. FSSAI / Food Licence

  • Food businesses may require registration or licence under the applicable food safety framework.
  • Category and licence requirements depend on the nature and scale of the food business.
  • Keep business, premises and food-category documents as applicable.
Official FoSCoS Portal ↗

7. Audit & Compliance

  • Audit involves examination of financial information and evidence to support an audit conclusion.
  • Maintain vouchers, ledgers, bank statements, confirmations and reconciliation working papers.
  • Tax audit applicability and reporting requirements depend on the applicable law and taxpayer facts.
Income Tax Official Portal ↗

8. Business & Registration

  • Company, LLP and partnership/firm setup involve different legal and registration requirements.
  • Typical compliance work may include PAN/TAN, GST, bookkeeping, return filing, TDS and annual filings.
  • For MCA services, use the official MCA portal and check current forms and fees.
