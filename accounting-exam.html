Accounting & Tax Practical — Practice Sheet

Shree Balaji Accounting • Smart Tax Solution • Adv. Pankaj Nokhwal

Exam se pehle neeche diye gaye 7 practical topics ki practice karein. Is sheet ko print karke handwritten working bhi kar sakte hain.

1. Journal Entries — 15 marks

Capital introduced ₹2,00,000 cash; goods purchased ₹35,000 + 18% GST; credit sales ₹60,000 + 18% GST; rent paid ₹12,000; customer received ₹25,000.

Task: Journal entries with CGST/SGST split and narration.

2. GST Tax Invoice — 15 marks

Goods taxable value ₹48,500; invoice discount ₹2,500 before tax; GST 18%; intra-state supply.

Task: Work out net taxable value, CGST, SGST and invoice total.

3. GSTR-2B Reconciliation — 20 marks

Invoice A-101: books and GSTR-2B taxable ₹10,000, CGST ₹900, SGST ₹900. Invoice B-204: books taxable ₹20,000, CGST/SGST ₹1,800 each; GSTR-2B taxable ₹18,000, CGST/SGST ₹1,620 each.

Task: Mark matched/mismatched invoices and calculate differences.

4. TDS Working — 15 marks

Assumed eligible contractor payment ₹72,000; for this question only use TDS rate 1% and assume no threshold exemption.

Task: Calculate TDS, net payment and journal entry. In real cases verify applicable section/rate.

5. Bank Reconciliation — 15 marks

Cash book bank balance ₹84,500; cheque issued not presented ₹12,000; cheque deposited not credited ₹8,500; bank charges not entered ₹650.

Task: Prepare BRS and show adjusted bank statement balance.

6. GST Return Review — 10 marks

Sales taxable value ₹5,00,000 (intra-state); eligible-looking ITC CGST ₹18,000 and SGST ₹18,000; one purchase invoice missing in GSTR-2B.

Task: List checks before GSTR-1/GSTR-3B and explain ITC verification.

7. Excel Reconciliation — 10 marks

Columns: Date, Party, GSTIN, Invoice No., Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST, Invoice Total, Difference, Status.

Task: Write total/difference formulas and explain mismatch filter and party-wise summary.

Submission Checklist

☐ Debit = Credit   ☐ GST arithmetic checked   ☐ Invoice-wise mismatch shown

☐ TDS assumptions stated   ☐ BRS adjustments shown   ☐ ITC eligibility verified

Note: This is practice material, not an official exam paper. Verify current tax rules before actual filing or deduction.
